## Scope
International recommendation to UNESCO Member States covering AI ethics across sectors, including education and research.

## Summary
The recommendation connects AI governance with human rights and dignity, transparency, fairness and human oversight across policy areas.

## Considerations
- Consider human oversight, fairness and inclusion when evaluating an AI-supported teaching workflow.

## Limitations
- A UNESCO recommendation is an international normative instrument, not a substitute for binding local law or university policy.
- The general principles need interpretation for the particular deployment and its users.
