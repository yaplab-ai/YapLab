## Scope
Web accessibility technical standard, not education-specific; applies to synchronized media as specified in the success criteria.

## Summary
WCAG 2.2 calls for captions for prerecorded synchronized media audio, subject to a labelled text-alternative exception, and for live synchronized media audio.

## Considerations
- Assess whether the material is synchronized media and provide captions appropriate to the applicable success criterion.

## Limitations
- A technical standard does not itself establish UAE legal duties; the US Title II rule separately incorporates WCAG 2.1 AA.
- The prerecorded criterion excludes media that is a clearly labelled media alternative for text. Captions alone do not establish overall accessibility.
