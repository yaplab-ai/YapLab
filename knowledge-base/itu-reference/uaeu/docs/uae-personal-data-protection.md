## Scope
Federal personal-data framework, subject to its territorial scope, exclusions and applicable regulations; potentially relevant when education tools process identifiable student or staff data.

## Summary
The federal law regulates personal-data processing and sets processing controls, individual rights and conditions for transfers, subject to its scope and exceptions.

## Considerations
- Determine whether the law and an exception apply before processing identifiable learner or staff information; identify the relevant processing basis and purpose.
- Consider data minimisation, security, data-subject rights and any applicable cross-border transfer conditions when selecting and operating a tool.

## Limitations
- Article 2 excludes government data and government entities processing personal data, certain regulated health and banking data, and free-zone establishments with their own data-protection legislation. Do not assume this federal regime applies to every university.
- Article references are starting points, not a complete account of duties, exceptions, implementing rules or later changes; verify the authoritative Arabic text and current instruments.
