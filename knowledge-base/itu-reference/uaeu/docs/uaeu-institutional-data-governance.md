## Scope
UAEU institutional data and people granted access to it. Data generated exclusively for scientific research is outside this policy.

## Summary
The policy assigns responsibilities for institutional data, including student records, classification, access and lifecycle governance.

## Considerations
- For a UAEU example, identify the data owner and classification before sharing teaching records or providing third-party access.
- Consult the institutional access, retention and lifecycle procedures and the responsible data-governance staff.

## Limitations
- This is a UAEU institutional policy, not a general determination of which federal privacy law applies.
- Research-only data requires separate research policies; the record supplies no universal retention period or incident deadline.
