## Scope
National guiding framework for development and use of AI; not an education-specific legal instrument.

## Summary
The charter describes principles including safety, algorithmic bias, privacy, transparency, human oversight and inclusive AI awareness.

## Considerations
- Use the charter as national policy context when considering human oversight, privacy and inclusive access.

## Limitations
- Guiding principles are not proof of a binding university-specific obligation, legal compliance or educational results.
