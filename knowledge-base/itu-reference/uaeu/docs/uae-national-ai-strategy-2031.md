## Scope
National strategic direction, including talent, research, infrastructure and AI governance. It does not itself approve a university tool or determine a legal duty.

## Summary
The strategy connects AI skills development, enabling infrastructure and responsible governance with national AI adoption.

## Considerations
- As strategic context, consider AI literacy and responsible human supervision when designing a teaching practice tool.
- Assess infrastructure and governance needs before proposing broader use; strategic alignment is not evidence of successful deployment.

## Limitations
- A national strategy is distinct from binding law, an institutional assessment policy or approval to process student information.
- The site includes historical initiatives and projections; no forecast or claim of current implementation is adopted here.
