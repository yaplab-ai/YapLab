## Scope
Non-binding guidance whose section 1.2 addresses AI systems making or informing significant decisions, including critical decisions. For ordinary teaching rehearsal, the guidelines are a voluntary design reference unless the actual use meets that decision scope.

## Summary
Ethical principles for decision-affecting AI, explicitly non-mandatory; distinct from the beta self-assessment tool.

## Considerations
- Distinguish ordinary rehearsal from AI that informs significant or critical decisions.
- Use the principles voluntarily for rehearsal design; revisit scope if outputs affect consequential assessment or decisions.

## Limitations
- Do not label these guidelines binding law, a certification or a general obligation for every rehearsal tool.
- Applicability to a particular deployment requires its actual purpose and effects.
