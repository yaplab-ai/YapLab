## Scope
Voluntary beta self-assessment for organisations developing or operating AI systems, based on the UAE AI Ethics Principles and Guidelines; not specific to education.

## Summary
The tool encourages assessment of ethical issues throughout an AI system's lifecycle, including data use, accountability, third-party methods and bias.

## Considerations
- Assess the intended teaching use, data-use risks, accountability, external dependencies and potential historical or technical bias.
- Revisit the assessment as the system changes and identify practical mitigations.

## Limitations
- The source calls its recommendations non-compulsory and the tool beta; a self-assessment score is not a government audit, approval or certification.
- General AI ethics guidance does not replace applicable law or university-specific policies.
