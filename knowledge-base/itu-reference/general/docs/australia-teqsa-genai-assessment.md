## Scope
Expert-developed, non-prescriptive guidance for Australian higher education on assessment in a generative-AI context.

## Summary
TEQSA’s expert paper offers guiding principles and propositions for assessment design that both assure learning and prepare students to engage ethically with generative AI.

## Considerations
- Review assessment design and assurance of learning alongside students’ ability to use AI responsibly.

## Limitations
- The paper describes directions for the sector, not a mandatory formula or a UAE policy.
- Related TEQSA resources appeared in 2025 and 2026; this entry is the 2023 paper.
