## Scope
Federal disability-rights law; its education provisions address education across institutions and include the Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research.

## Summary
The education provisions address equal educational opportunities for persons with disabilities and the role of education authorities in meeting their needs.

## Considerations
- Consider access for learners with disabilities when preparing learning materials and selecting educational tools.
- Consult university disability-support staff about appropriate support and the applicable institutional processes.
