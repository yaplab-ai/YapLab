## Scope
Ministry overview of inclusive education and its 2020 policy for government and private schools following the Ministry curriculum.

## Summary
The Ministry describes removing learning barriers, tailored support and assistive technology as parts of inclusive education in the school context.

## Considerations
- Use barrier removal and suitable accessible formats as design considerations when adapting educational materials.

## Limitations
- The cited 2020 policy is school-specific and should not be represented as a university obligation; consult higher-education and institution-specific rules.
- The overview is a summary and does not establish that an individual tool or lesson is accessible.
