## Scope
EU AI law covering specified AI system providers and deployers; education and training at all levels are in Annex III, with role, territorial and high-risk classification conditions.

## Summary
The Act lists AI systems intended to evaluate learning outcomes among education high-risk use cases, subject to Article 6 classification conditions. Article 4 requires providers and deployers to support development of AI literacy.

## Considerations
- Check intended purpose and Article 6(3) before characterising an education system as high risk.
- Consider context, staff knowledge and the affected people in measures supporting AI literacy.

## Limitations
- EU law is a comparative example, not a UAE obligation; not all educational AI or formative feedback is automatically high risk.
- The Commission states that Annex III high-risk rules apply from 2 December 2027. Article 4 applied from 2 February 2025 and was amended in 2026.
