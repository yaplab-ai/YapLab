## Scope
Article 3 covers federal entities and specified regulated or government-connected parties; local, semi-government and private entities are also asked to consider it with best efforts. Coverage of a particular private university is not established.

## Summary
A scoped digital-services policy. Annex 1 specifies WCAG 2.1 Level AA for public-service sites within that policy context.

## Considerations
- Check the entity, digital service and regulatory/contracting relationship against Article 3 before asserting applicability.
- For covered public-service sites, examine Annex 1 section 1; elsewhere treat it as a design reference pending applicable-policy review.

## Limitations
- Do not assert blanket applicability to all private universities or all classroom activities.
- A citation or automated review does not establish WCAG conformance or legal compliance.
