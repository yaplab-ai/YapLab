## Scope
International policy and practice guidance for generative AI in education and research, including teachers, learners and researchers.

## Summary
UNESCO advocates a human-centred approach to GenAI that protects privacy and human agency, evaluates risks and aligns pedagogical use with educational needs.

## Considerations
- Critically review generated educational content for errors, bias and cultural context before use.
- Assess privacy, inclusion and educational value when designing or adopting a GenAI-supported teaching activity.

## Limitations
- International guidance is not by itself UAE legislation or university policy.
- The 2023 publication predates later model and policy developments; verify current local rules and institutional guidance.
