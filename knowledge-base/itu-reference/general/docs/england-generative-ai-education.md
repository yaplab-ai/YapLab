## Scope
England education-sector position, principally aimed at schools and colleges; the Department notes that some considerations are relevant to other stages.

## Summary
The guidance describes possible teacher-facing uses and advises professional judgement over AI outputs, data protection and attention to intellectual property and assessment integrity.

## Considerations
- Have an educator check generated material for accuracy and suitability before sharing or using it.
- Consider the privacy and ownership of material entered into a tool and the rules governing assessment.

## Limitations
- This is an England example, focused on schools and colleges; its legal statements do not establish UAE requirements or university assessment rules.
- The guidance is updated over time; the linked page was last updated on 12 August 2025.
